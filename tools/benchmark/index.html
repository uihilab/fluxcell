Solver throughput benchmark

Compares the multi-pass MUSCL kernels (five dispatches and one scalar readback per step) with the fused tiled kernel, submitted one step at a time and in GPU-resident batches. Each run warms up 20 steps, then times 30 batches. Times are wall-clock to queue completion (readback included); there is no rendering and no CPU reference. Numerical correctness is established separately by the validation suite.

Ready. Requires WebGPU on localhost or HTTPS.
