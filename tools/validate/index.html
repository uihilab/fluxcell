Shallow-water numerical validation

Runs the new WebGPU core against analytical equilibria, water budgets, and an independent f64 CPU implementation. No maps or rendering are required.

Fifteen 1,000-step cases (including friction, sources, obstacles, open and outflow boundaries), 21 edge-size cases and five repeatability runs. MUSCL methods also run smooth-wave refinement and exact dam-break comparisons; the fused kernel is also compared step by step with the multi-pass MUSCL kernel.
